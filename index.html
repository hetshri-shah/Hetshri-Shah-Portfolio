About me

Curious about circuits, machines and clean ideas

I'm pursuing a B.Tech in Electronics & Communication Engineering with a VLSI specialization at Karnavati University (UIT) in Gandhinagar. Before that I completed my CBSE schooling at Gujarat Public School, finishing high school with a 9.4 CGPA and senior secondary with 9.1.
